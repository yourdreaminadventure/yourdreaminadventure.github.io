Role Model: Douglas Bader

Douglas Bader was a British pilot in the Royal Air Force, known for his great courage and determination.

Bader’s story is one of the most remarkable in the world of aviation, not only because of his incredible flying skills, but also for his courage and resilience in the face of adversity.

He lost both of his legs before joining the Royal Air Force, yet he never abandoned his determination or dreams. After a long recovery, he returned with unwavering resolve to undergo flight training once again. With great willpower, he successfully passed his check flights and pursued the path to regain his position as a fighter pilot.
