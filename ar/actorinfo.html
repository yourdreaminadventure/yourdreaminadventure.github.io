🎬 My Dream: Actor – Command Plan
📋 Daily Commands
Practice Acting → Read stories and act out the characters.
Build Confidence → Speak loudly and clearly in front of a mirror.
Stay Healthy → Exercise, eat well, and rest — strong body, strong performance.
📅 Weekly Commands
Watch & Learn → Study movies, plays, or shows and notice how actors perform.
Practice Emotions → Try acting happy, sad, angry, or surprised in different ways.
Record Yourself → Make short videos to see and improve your acting.
🎯 Long-Term Commands
Join Drama/School Plays → Get stage experience.
Learn Communication → Practice speaking skills and body language.
Study Great Actors → Read about their journey and techniques.
Never Give Up → Remember: I am training to shine on stage and screen. 🌟🎥
