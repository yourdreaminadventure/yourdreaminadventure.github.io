📚 My Dream: Teacher – Command Plan
📋 Daily Commands
Study Hard → Focus on all subjects, especially the ones you want to teach.
Practice Explaining → Teach what you learn to a friend, sibling, or even a toy.
Stay Patient & Kind → A good teacher listens and helps others.
📅 Weekly Commands
Read Books → Stories, history, and science to grow your knowledge.
Help Classmates → Support friends with homework or questions.
Improve Communication → Practice speaking clearly and confidently.
🎯 Long-Term Commands
Aim for Good Grades → Especially in education-related subjects.
Learn Leadership → Join school clubs or activities to guide others.
Study Education Methods → Read or watch how teachers explain and manage classrooms.
Never Give Up → Remember: I am training to inspire and teach the next generation. 🌟
