Role Model: Zaha Hadid

Zaha Hadid, the British-Iraqi architect

Zaha is not only one of the most famous architects in the world but also the first woman to receive the Pritzker Architecture Prize in 2004, in addition
to many other awards. She was described as the “Queen of Curves” in architecture, and it was said that she liberated architecture from its constraints and gave it a new, more dynamic character. Her works include the Aquatics Centre built for the 2012 London Olympics, the University of Michigan Museum in the United States, and the Guangzhou Opera House in China
