Role Model: Mostafa Refaat

Captain Mostafa Refaat

Captain Mostafa Refaat was a brave police officer in the Battle of Ismailia,

71 years ago. He and his friends in the police fought the British soldiers with great courage.

Their bravery made this day very special, and now it is called Egyptian Police Day.One of his famous words to the British general was:

“Even if we fight for 50 years, we will never give up”
