Role Model: Ahmed Hassan Zewail

Ahmed Zewail was an Egyptian- chemist who won the 1999 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for his research in femtochemistry.

He invented a microscope that photographs laser beams in femtoseconds ,

thus enabling molecules to be seen during chemical reactions .

He is considered a pioneer of femtochemistry and is known as the "Father of Femtochemistry." He was a professor of chemistry and physics at
the California Institute of Technology .
