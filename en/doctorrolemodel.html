Role Model: Magdy Yacoub

Dr. Magdi Yacoub: The Knight of the World’s Most Critical Heart Surgeries

Sir Magdi Yacoub is considered one of the most famous heart surgeons in the world. He entered the Guinness World Records with his patient, John McCafferty, as the longest-living person after a heart transplant surgery, surpassing the previous record of 30 years, 11 months, and 10 days. He also took part in 25,000 operations. Under his leadership, the Harefield Hospital organ transplant program began in 1980, and within just 10 years, he accomplished 1,000 surgeries, making it one of the leading transplant centers in the United Kingdom.
