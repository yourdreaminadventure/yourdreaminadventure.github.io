PHOTOGRAPHER

📸 My Dream: Photographer – Command Plan
📋 Daily Commands
Practice Daily → Take at least one photo every day.
Observe the World → Look for light, colors, and interesting details around you.
Stay Creative → Try new angles, shadows, and backgrounds.
📅 Weekly Commands
Review Your Photos → Choose your best shots and think about how to improve.
Learn Photography Skills → Watch tutorials about lighting, editing, and camera settings.
Experiment → Take photos of nature, people, objects, or buildings.
🎯 Long-Term Commands
Build a Portfolio → Save your best photos to show your progress.
Learn Editing Tools → Practice with apps like Photoshop or Lightroom.
Join Photography Clubs/Competitions → Share your work and learn from others.
Never Give Up → Remember: I am training to capture the world through my lens. 🌍✨
