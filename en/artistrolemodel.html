Leonardo da Vinci

The first name to shine in the history of that craft was the Italian polymath painter Leonardo da Vinci.

Da Vinci was a painter, engineer, sculptor, architect, cartographer, and scientific publisher.

He is one of the most famous scholars of the Renaissance and is considered one of the greatest geniuses in the history of humanity as a whole.

He was often described as having an “unparalleled creative imagination.” One of his most famous works is the Mona Lisa.
