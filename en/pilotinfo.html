PILOT

Pilot Dream Commands

Daily commands: Study aviation, science, and mathematics. Practice English (the international language of pilots). Exercise and play sports to stay strong and healthy. Use simulator for practice. Read books or watch documentaries about aviation. Save money for training.

Weekly commands : Visit or contact a flight school or airport. Listen to ATC conversations. Join aviation groups or forums. Revise notes and test yourself. Track and update your budget.

Long-Term commands: Get Class 1 Medical Certificate. Complete PPL. Continue with CPL, IFR, and Multi-Engine training. Build 1500+ flying hours. Apply for cadet or airline programs. Progress from co-pilot to captain.
