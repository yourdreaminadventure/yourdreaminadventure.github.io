Firefighter

To achieve your dream as a firefighter :

Daily Command

Plan Commands Train Body → Do push-ups, running, climbing, and stretching.

Stay Healthy → Eat strong food, drink water, and get enough sleep.

Learn Safety → Read about fire safety rules and first aid.

Weekly Commands

Practice Discipline → Follow a routine with study + training.

Help Others → Always be kind, brave, and ready to assist.

Improve Teamwork → Work with friends/family in group tasks. Long-Term Commands

Build Strength & Endurance → Train to carry heavy things and run long distances.

Study Science → Focus on chemistry (fire, heat, materials) and health.

Join Scouts or Sports → Practice courage, teamwork, and leadership.

Never Give Up → Remember: I am training to save lives.
