Role Model: Lionel Messi

Full Name: Lionel Andrés Messi

Born: June 24, 1987 – Rosario, Argentina

Position: Forward / Right Winger

Clubs:
Barcelona (2004–2021) – club legend

Paris Saint-Germain (PSG) (2021–2023)

Currently plays for Inter Miami (USA)

National Team: Captain of Argentina, won the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar and Copa América 2021.

Individual Achievements: Won the Ballon d’Or (Best Player in the World) 8 times – a record.

Team Achievements: 4× UEFA Champions League, 10× La Liga, plus many other domestic and international trophies.
