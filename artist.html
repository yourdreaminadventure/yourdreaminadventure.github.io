ARTIST

Artist Dream Commands

Daily commands: Practice drawing or painting. Study art basics (shapes, colors, perspective). Learn from online tutorials or courses. Explore creativity by sketching something new. Read art books or watch documentaries about artists. Keep a sketchbook and draw every day.

Weekly commands: Visit art galleries or museums (online or in person). Share your artwork with friends or online communities. Join an art group or forum. Review your progress and set new goals. Try a new style, tool, or technique.

Long-Term commands : Build a strong art portfolio. Learn digital tools (Photoshop, Procreate, Illustrator, etc.). Take art courses or workshops. Enter art contests or exhibitions. Sell or showcase art online. Develop your own style and become a professional artist.
