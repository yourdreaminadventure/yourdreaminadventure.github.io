Engineer

To achieve your dream as an Engineer :

Daily commands: Study Hard Focus on math, physics, and problem-solving. Think Like a Builder , Ask “how does this work?” about machines, bridges, or gadgets. Practice Drawing/Designing . Sketch simple machines, houses, or ideas.

Weekly commands: Do Small Projects , Build with LEGO, cardboard, or simple electronics. Solve Puzzles , Play logic, coding, or building games. Learn Technology , Try beginner coding (Scratch, Python) or design tools.

Long-Term commands: Aim for Top Grades,Especially in math, physics, and computer science. Join Science/Engineering Clubs , Work on team projects and competitions. Research Fields of Engineering → Civil, mechanical, electrical, software, etc. Never Give Up , Remember: I am training to invent, build, and solve real-world problems.
